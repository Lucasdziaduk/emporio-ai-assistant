# M4 — Retrieval evaluation report

**Evaluation corpus:** supplied Empório da Música internal policy manual, version 2.1 (June 2025).

**Golden set:** 24 manually authored Portuguese queries. Each query has one or more expected section identifiers.

## Results

| Metric | Observed result |
|---|---:|
| Number of cases | 24 |
| Retrieval cutoff | Top 3 |
| Hits | 24 / 24 |
| Misses | 0 |
| Recall@3 | 1.0000 |
| Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) | 0.9792 |

Recall@3 measures whether at least one expected section appears among the first three results. MRR measures the reciprocal rank of the first expected section and averages it over all queries.

These numbers apply only to this small, document-specific golden set. They do not mean the retriever will answer every future question correctly, and they are not a statistically representative estimate of production accuracy. The normalized `relevance_score` is relative within each query and is not a calibrated probability.

## Evaluation limitations

- The test queries were authored from the same eight-page policy manual they evaluate.
- Section-level matching does not validate the natural-language answer generated by an LLM.
- Retrieval quality does not resolve policy conflicts; unresolved cases remain flagged for human review.
- New formulations, typos, unseen synonyms, and future manual revisions require additional test cases.
